Game Setup

One screen, turn-based play. On your turn you pick a clue. The host clicks Correct, Incorrect, or Pass.

Pass = no penalty, but the lowest-score other player gets a single steal attempt. After each clue, turn advances to the next player.

Host Tips

- Turn on the built-in timer from the top-right controls if the room wants stricter play.

- "Expected response" is shown when you hit Reveal Response.

- You can start a fresh randomized board any time using New Game (New Questions).

This version now runs from a committed local question bank with strict validation and save-safe round loading. Double Round is harder than Round One; Final Clue is hard but fair.
